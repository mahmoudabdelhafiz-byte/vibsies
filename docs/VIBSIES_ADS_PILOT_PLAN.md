# Vibsies Ads pilot checklist

Status: Planning only, not authorized for production.

## Scope

Run a consent-based nonfinancial pilot in staging with two publisher accounts from different audience categories, one advertiser and one administrator. Use simulated pricing, with financial posting disabled. Do not promise payments or publish commercial advertisements without separate authorization.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Publisher onboarding is optional and does not require promoting Vibsies.
- [ ] Ownership challenge codes, manual review and audit records work.
- [ ] Matching prioritizes relevant city and category over follower count.
- [ ] Advertisers can send offers, and publishers can accept or decline.
- [ ] Publication evidence, disputes and cancellations remain reviewable.
- [ ] Both language choices and mobile layouts work across all five Ads screens.
- [ ] Users cannot access campaigns, offers or publisher properties they do not own.
- [ ] No real payment, payout or financial credit is generated.
- [ ] Independent privacy and security review completed.
- [ ] Database backup, rollback drill and release sign-off completed before production activation.

## Test evidence to capture

Record reproducible issues and screenshots from voluntary pilot sessions. Compare a geographically relevant campaign against an unrelated category and document differences without claims of guaranteed engagement.

Production flags stay disabled pending review.
